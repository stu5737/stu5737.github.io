一、誰是這份政策的對象

Solefood（以下稱「本 App」）是一款以真實步行 GPS 軌跡驅動的遊戲。本政策說明本 App 收集哪些資料、為什麼收集、如何儲存與分享，以及你可以如何控制這些資料。

本 App 由 Yuming Liao（個人開發者）開發與維運,聯絡信箱:
stepnxadidas@gmail.com

二、我們收集的資料

本 App 為了核心玩法與遊戲穩定性,會收集以下類別的資料:

2.1 精確 GPS 位置與軌跡(必要)

  • 收集內容:經緯度、時間戳、GPS 精度、行動速度、行動方向
  • 收集時機:採集模式啟動後持續收集(前景 + 背景)
  • 為什麼:
    • 計算你的步行距離(遊戲核心進度指標)
    • 在真實地圖上呈現你的採集路徑
    • 偵測你進入特定區域以觸發採集事件
    • 偵測 GPS 模擬器、車輛速度等作弊行為
  • 儲存位置:
    • 本機 SQLite 資料庫(gps_trail.db):永久保留軌跡點供軌跡重繪
    • 雲端伺服器:加密上傳,與你的用戶代號(SF-XXXXXX)關聯
  • 保留期間:帳號活躍期間;刪除帳號後 30 日內清除

2.2 步數與活動類型(必要)

  • 收集內容:每分鐘步數、活動狀態(walking / running / vehicle / stationary)
  • 收集時機:採集模式期間
  • 為什麼:反作弊。驗證你的移動是真實步行而非車輛或 GPS 模擬。
  • 儲存位置:僅本機處理,不上傳原始感測資料;只上傳「是否作弊」的二元判定結果作為遊戲事件
  • iOS 來源:HealthKit + Core Motion
  • Android 來源:Activity Recognition API + Sensor API

2.3 HealthKit Workout Session(iOS 限定,必要)

  • 收集內容:本 App 主動「建立」一個 Workout Session,類型為 Outdoor Walk
  • 收集時機:採集模式啟動時建立、結束時關閉
  • 為什麼:HealthKit Workout Session 讓 iOS 系統知道使用者正在運動,保持 App 在背景活躍,避免被 Jetsam 機制終止
  • 儲存位置:僅本機儲存(Apple 健康 App 內),本 App 不讀取也不上傳
  • 可選:你可以在 Apple 健康 App → 共享 → 應用程式中關閉此授權;關閉後採集模式會改用備援機制(背景採集成功率會略降)

2.4 相機拍攝的食材照片(選用)

  • 收集內容:你主動觸發相機拍攝的食材照片
  • 收集時機:你在卸貨流程內主動按下「拍照」按鈕時
  • 為什麼:作為遊戲採集紀錄與活動兌獎憑證
  • 儲存位置:上傳至 Solefood 雲端伺服器,與你的用戶代號關聯
  • 不收集:相簿內的其他照片、EXIF 資訊(如位置)

2.5 用戶代號與設定(必要)

  • 收集內容:
    • 用戶代號(首次安裝隨機產生,格式 SF-XXXXXX)
    • 遊戲設定偏好(語言、教學完成標記)
    • 帳號綁定資訊(僅在你主動綁定 email 後)
  • 儲存位置:本機 AsyncStorage(裝置端);帳號綁定後同步至雲端伺服器
  • 為什麼:辨識同一裝置的回訪使用者、跨裝置同步進度(綁定後)

2.6 崩潰與錯誤診斷資料(選用 / 第三方)

  • 收集內容:App 崩潰堆疊追蹤、錯誤日誌、裝置型號、OS 版本、GPS session metadata(不含座標)
  • 收集時機:App 發生崩潰或關鍵錯誤時
  • 為什麼:修復 App 問題、提升穩定性
  • 傳輸對象:Sentry(Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io)
    • Sentry 隱私政策:https://sentry.io/privacy/
  • 儲存位置:Sentry 雲端,與你的用戶代號關聯(匿名化處理,不包含 email 或真實姓名)
  • 可選 / 預設關閉:本 App 預設關閉錯誤回報;需在「設定 → 崩潰與錯誤回報」主動開啟才會上傳

三、我們不會做的事

  • 不會販售你的任何資料給第三方廣告商
  • 不會做跨 App 追蹤(NSPrivacyTracking: false)
  • 不會讀取你的聯絡人、通話紀錄、簡訊、瀏覽紀錄
  • 不會錄音(麥克風權限僅供未來語音功能保留,目前未使用)
  • 不會讀取相簿內的其他照片(僅相機拍攝即時上傳)
  • 不會在沒有你明確同意的情況下顯示廣告

1. Who This Policy Applies To

Solefood (hereinafter "the App") is a walking game driven by real-world GPS trails. This policy describes what data the App collects, why it collects them, how they are stored and shared, and how you can control them.

The App is developed and operated by Yuming Liao (independent developer). Contact: stepnxadidas@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

The App collects the following data for core gameplay and stability:

2.1 Precise GPS Location and Trail (Required)

  • What: Latitude/longitude, timestamps, GPS accuracy, speed, heading
  • When: Continuously during foraging mode (foreground + background)
  • Why:
    • Calculate walking distance (core gameplay progress)
    • Render your foraging path on the in-game map
    • Trigger zone-based foraging events
    • Detect GPS spoofing, vehicle-speed cheating
  • Stored at: On-device SQLite (gps_trail.db) — kept for trail replay; Cloud server — encrypted, linked to your user ID (SF-XXXXXX)
  • Retention: While the account is active; deleted within 30 days of account deletion

2.2 Step Count and Activity Type (Required)

  • What: Steps per minute, activity state (walking / running / vehicle / stationary)
  • When: During foraging mode
  • Why: Anti-cheat. Verify that movement is real walking, not vehicle or GPS simulator.
  • Stored at: On-device only. Raw sensor data is never uploaded. Only the boolean "cheat detected" result is sent as a game event.

2.3 HealthKit Workout Session (iOS only, Required)

  • What: The App creates a Workout Session (type: Outdoor Walk)
  • Why: Keeps iOS aware that the user is exercising, preventing Jetsam from terminating the App in background
  • Stored at: On-device only (in Apple Health app). The App does not read your Health data or upload it.

2.4 Camera Photos of Foraged Items (Optional)

  • What: Photos you actively capture during the unload flow
  • Why: Foraging records and event reward proofs
  • Stored at: Uploaded to Solefood cloud servers, linked to your user ID
  • Not collected: Other photos in your library; EXIF metadata (including location)

2.5 User ID and Preferences (Required)

User ID, game preferences, account binding info. Stored on-device (AsyncStorage); synced to cloud only after account binding.

2.6 Crash and Error Diagnostics (Optional / Third Party)

  • Sent to: Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io)
  • Optional / Off by default: Crash reporting is OFF by default; you must actively enable it in Settings → Crash and Error Reports.

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do NOT sell your data to third-party advertisers
  • We do NOT track you across other apps (NSPrivacyTracking: false)
  • We do NOT read your contacts, call logs, SMS, or browsing history
  • We do NOT record audio (microphone permission reserved for future voice features, currently unused)
  • We do NOT read other photos in your library (only camera-captured photos, uploaded in real time)
  • We do NOT show ads without your explicit consent

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights (per GDPR / CCPA / Taiwan PDPA):

  • Right of access: Request access to all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your account and all linked data (within 30 days)
  • Right to data portability: Request export of your game data as JSON
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw prior consent (some permissions, once revoked, will prevent the App from continuing to forage)

Contact: email stepnxadidas@gmail.com with subject "Solefood Privacy Request"

6. Minors

The App does not market to children under 13 and does not collect data specifically about children. If you believe a minor is using the App without consent, please contact us to delete their data.

7. $WaWa Tokens and In-Game Currency Disclaimer

  • $WaWa is purely an in-game token used only by game mechanics
  • Cannot be purchased with real money
  • Cannot be exchanged for real currency or physical goods
  • Cannot be transferred to other users
  • Internal valuation calculations are game-balance parameters only and do not represent any monetary value

8. Notice of Changes

When we update this policy, we will display an in-app notice and ask you to review. Material changes (e.g., new data categories, new third-party recipients) require fresh, active consent.

9. Disputes

If you have concerns about this policy, please first email stepnxadidas@gmail.com. If you cannot reach a satisfactory resolution, you may file a complaint under the applicable law of your jurisdiction (Taiwan / your country of residence).
