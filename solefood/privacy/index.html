Solefood（以下稱「本 App」）是一款以真實步行 GPS 軌跡驅動的遊戲。本政策說明本 App 收集哪些資料、為什麼收集、如何儲存與分享，以及你可以如何控制這些資料。

二、我們收集的資料

本 App 為了核心玩法與遊戲穩定性,會收集以下類別的資料:

2.1 精確 GPS 位置與軌跡(必要)

  • 收集內容:經緯度、時間戳、GPS 精度、行動速度、行動方向
  • 收集時機:採集模式啟動後持續收集(前景 + 背景)
  • 為什麼:
    • 計算你的步行距離(遊戲核心進度指標)
    • 在真實地圖上呈現你的採集路徑
    • 偵測你進入特定區域以觸發採集事件
    • 偵測 GPS 模擬器、車輛速度等作弊行為
    • 好友位置轉播(2026-09-18 補登記):你主動開始一段分享時,你選的收件者會看到你目前的位置;分享一結束(或超過 12 小時上限),伺服器上那段分享的座標就會被清空,整段紀錄 7 天後刪除。這段期間伺服器會透過 Expo 推播服務送出通知,內容包含你的暱稱與縣市或地點級的描述(例如「我現在在臺北市採集」),見第四節。
  • 誰看得到:只有你自己,以及你主動開始分享時所選的收件者(你隨時可以結束分享或封鎖對方,封鎖後推播立刻停止)。營運維運者可在內部儀表板看到近期採集場次的起點,用於除錯與反作弊;該頁反查地名時會把四捨五入到約 110 公尺的概略座標送給 OpenStreetMap,見第四節。
  • 儲存位置(裝置):本機 SQLite 資料庫 gps_trail.db,用於重畫你走過的路線
  • 儲存位置(我們的伺服器)(2026-09-18 更正:原文把「有上傳」寫成「沒有上傳」,與實際行為不符;現行說法如下):會經 HTTPS 加密上傳,並與你的玩家代碼(以及綁定後的帳號)關聯:
    • 採集期間每 30 秒上傳一次目前座標與累計距離
    • 每一趟的起點與終點座標、總距離、時長
    • 為什麼要上傳:里程是排行榜與 $WaWa 經濟的核心數值,若只信任裝置端回報的數字, 任何人改一下手機時間或距離就能造假。伺服器端獨立記錄的里程是反作弊上限的唯一來源。 這一項無法關閉——關掉等於無法參與排行榜與防作弊機制。
  • 保留期間(裝置):留存於裝置直到你刪除 App、手動清除 App 資料,或在設定內開啟自動修剪
  • 保留期間(伺服器):不自動刪除——你走過的路線、每一趟的起訖點與統計 都會一直留著。為什麼不刪:這是你的歷史紀錄,而你登出或換帳號時這支手機上的那一份 會被清掉(換人用同一支手機時不該看到前一個人去過哪),所以伺服器上這一份是唯一的一份—— 設一個到期日就等於到期後把你走過的路刪掉。你可以隨時要求刪除(見第五節);刪除帳號時會一併刪除。
  • 保留期間(伺服器・起訖座標):每一趟的起點與終點座標目前會一直留著,跟路線、距離、時長一樣不會自動刪除。以後如果要改成定期清除,我們會先在這一頁公告,再開始清。你隨時可以要求刪除(見第五節);刪除帳號時會一併刪除。

1. Who This Policy Applies To

Solefood (hereinafter "the App") is a walking game driven by real-world GPS trails. This policy describes what data the App collects, why it collects them, how they are stored and shared, and how you can control them.

The App is developed and operated by Yuming Liao (independent developer). Contact: stepnxadidas@gmail.com

2. Data We Collect

The App collects the following data for core gameplay and stability:

2.1 Precise GPS Location and Trail (Required)

  • What: Latitude/longitude, timestamps, GPS accuracy, speed, heading
  • When: Continuously during foraging mode (foreground + background)
  • Why:
    • Calculate walking distance (core gameplay progress)
    • Render your foraging path on the in-game map
    • Trigger zone-based foraging events
    • Detect GPS spoofing, vehicle-speed cheating
    • Live sharing with friends (added to this policy 2026-09-18): when you actively start a sharing session, the recipients you picked can see where you are. When the session ends (or hits the 12-hour cap) the server blanks that session's coordinates, and the record is deleted after 7 days. During the session the server sends notifications through the Expo push service containing your nickname and a county- or place-level description (e.g. "I'm foraging in Taipei") — see section 4.
  • Who can see it: you, and the recipients you pick when you start a sharing session (you can end the session or block someone at any time; blocking stops the push immediately). Operations staff can see recent session start points on an internal dashboard for debugging and anti-cheat; when that page resolves a point to a place name it sends coordinates rounded to roughly 110 metres to OpenStreetMap — see section 4.
  • Stored at (device): Local SQLite database gps_trail.db, used to redraw your route
  • Stored at (our server) (corrected 2026-09-18 — the previous wording denied an upload that does in fact happen): Uploaded over HTTPS (encrypted in transit), linked to your player code (and to your account once bound):
    • Your current coordinates and cumulative distance, uploaded every 30 seconds while foraging
    • The start and end coordinates, total distance and duration of each outing
    • Why we upload it: distance drives the leaderboard and the in-game economy. If we trusted only device-reported numbers, anyone could fake them. The server’s own record is the sole basis of our anti-cheat distance cap. This upload cannot be turned off — turning it off would mean no leaderboard and no anti-cheat.
  • Retention (device): Kept until you delete the app, clear app data, or enable auto-pruning in Settings
  • Retention (server): Not auto-deleted — your routes, per-outing start/end points and statistics are kept. Why we don't delete them: this is your own history, and the copy on this phone is wiped when you sign out or switch accounts (whoever uses the phone next must not see where you have been), so the server copy is the only one — an expiry date would mean deleting the paths you walked. You can request deletion at any time (see section 5), and everything is removed when you delete your account.
  • Retention (server — start/end coordinates): each outing’s start and end coordinates are currently kept and are not auto-deleted, the same as your route, distance and duration. If we ever decide to clear them on a schedule, we will announce it on this page before we start. You can request deletion at any time (see section 5), and they are removed when you delete your account.
  • What you can control: “Places that aren’t recorded” (Settings → Privacy) lets you designate up to 3 locations; items picked up inside those circles are not written to your map record. The circles themselves are never uploaded and exist only on this phone.

2.2 Step Count and Activity Type (Required)

  • What: Steps per minute, activity state (walking / running / vehicle / stationary)
  • When: During foraging mode
  • Why: Anti-cheat. Verify that movement is real walking, not vehicle or GPS simulator.
  • Stored at: On-device only. Raw sensor data is never uploaded. Only the boolean "cheat detected" result is sent as a game event.

2.3 HealthKit Workout Session (iOS only, Required)

  • What: The App creates a Workout Session (type: Outdoor Walk)
  • Why: Keeps iOS aware that the user is exercising, preventing Jetsam from terminating the App in background
  • Stored at: On-device only (in Apple Health app). The App does not read your Health data or upload it.

  • Picking from your library: a message-board post can attach one photo from your library; that photo likewise stays on your phone. Picking goes through the system photo picker, so the App never gains access to your whole library.

Menu photos (not yet enabled)

  • Status: this feature is currently off (disabled server-side, and there is no entry point in the App).
  • What it would collect once enabled (written down in advance — it will not start quietly): the original menu photo file you choose to upload (including its EXIF metadata), the capture time, the coordinates at capture time, and your user ID.
  • Purpose: building restaurant menu data; photos are human-reviewed.
  • Deletion: removed when you delete your account.

2.5 User ID and Preferences (Required)

User ID and game preferences, stored on-device (AsyncStorage); synced to the cloud after account binding.

Account binding also collects your email address and name — provided by Apple or Google when you sign in with them (Apple Sign-In requests the full-name and email scopes), or entered by you when registering with an email address. Handled via Firebase Authentication (Google). Your player nickname is also collected and is shown to other players on leaderboards, in wagers and in restaurant reviews.

2.7 Cloud Save (after account binding)

  • What: the map area you have walked (fog-of-war hex set, up to 30,000 hex ids), your collection index, lifetime distance, lifetime $WaWa, inventory contents, nickname, and the number of “places that aren’t recorded” you have set (count only — never the locations)
  • Why: to restore your progress on a new phone or after reinstalling
  • Stored at: Solefood cloud servers, linked to your account
  • Retention: for the life of the account; deleted when you delete your account

2.6 Crash and Error Diagnostics (Optional / Third Party)

  • Sent to: Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io)
  • Optional / Off by default: Crash reporting is OFF by default; you must actively enable it in Settings → Crash and Error Reports, and while it is off such an error goes nowhere at all. You can turn it back off at any time.
  • Honest boundary (added 2026-09-18): that toggle governs the App on your phone. When our server-side API itself errors, our server also sends an event to Sentry containing the requesting user ID and uid (coordinates are redacted); that path is not governed by the toggle — it is our own system log, not data from your device.
